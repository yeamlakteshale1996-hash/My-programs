About us

GospelWave is an online music player dedicated to Ethiopian gospel music. The website features talented gospel singers including Bereket Tesfaye, Dawit Getachew, Mesfine Mamo, Illash Fikadu, Aster Abebe, Pastor Tesfaye Gabiso, Tesfaye Chala, and Kingdom Worship Ministry Users can explore artists, discover gospel songs, create playlists, and enjoy music that inspires faith, hope, and worship. The website aims to provide a simple, modern, and enjoyable platform for Ethiopian gospel music lovers.
